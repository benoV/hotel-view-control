PRIVACY

Your hotel preferences remain on your device.

Hotel View Control stores a hotel identifier, hotel name, optional canonical property URL, and your chosen visibility state in Chrome extension local storage on your device.

What we do not collect

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store your personal information, browsing history, hotel preferences, or behavior on an external server. It has no accounts, analytics, tracking, remote code, or external database.

What the extension changes

Hotel View Control changes only the local visual display of supported hotel search-result cards. It does not change prices, ratings, availability, booking controls, checkout, payments, reviews, rankings, or advertisements.
