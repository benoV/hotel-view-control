PRIVACY

Your hotel preferences remain on your device.

Hotel View Control processes the displayed hotel name, property identifier, and optional canonical property URL for a hotel result you choose to hide or dim. It stores that information with your chosen visibility state in Chrome extension local storage on your device.

How we use and share data

This local information is used only to apply and restore your chosen hotel-card display state. It is never transmitted to King Tide Media or any other party, and it is not sold, shared, used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond this extension’s local hotel-list controls.

Your choices

Before Hotel View Control starts saving preferences, it asks for your confirmation in the extension interface. You can restore individual hotels, clear preferences for one site, or clear all preferences at any time from the extension controls.

What we do not operate

The extension has no accounts, analytics, tracking, remote code, or external database. It does not send your hotel preferences, browsing activity, or hotel-card data to an external server.

What the extension changes

Hotel View Control changes only the local visual display of supported hotel search-result cards. It does not change prices, ratings, availability, booking controls, checkout, payments, reviews, rankings, or advertisements.
