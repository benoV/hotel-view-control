SUPPORT

Simple controls for hotel search results.

Getting started

Open a supported hotel search-results page and use the small control over a hotel image to hide, dim, or restore that listing. Click the extension icon in the browser toolbar to review and restore saved preferences.

Supported sites

Hotel View Control currently supports Booking.com and Agoda search-result pages. The extension is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Booking.com, Agoda, Loom, or their parent companies.

Privacy

Hotel preferences are stored locally in Chrome extension storage and are not transmitted externally. Read the complete privacy statement.
