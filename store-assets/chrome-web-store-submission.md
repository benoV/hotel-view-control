## Single purpose

Let users locally hide, dim, and restore hotel search-result cards on Booking.com and Agoda.

## Short description

Locally hide or dim hotel results you have already reviewed on Booking.com and Agoda.

## Detailed description

Hotel View Control makes hotel search results easier to manage. Hide a result you no longer need to see, or dim one that you may want to revisit. Restore any saved hotel whenever you want.

Your preferences are stored only in your browser and are reapplied while you browse supported hotel search pages. The extension changes only your local view of result cards; it never changes prices, ratings, availability, booking actions, checkout, payments, reviews, rankings, or advertisements.

Hotel View Control is independent and is not affiliated with Booking.com, Agoda, or their parent companies.

## Privacy declaration guidance

Hotel View Control processes the displayed hotel name, property identifier, and optional property URL only when a user chooses to hide or dim a listing. That information and the visibility preference are stored locally in Chrome on the user’s device. They are never transmitted, sold, shared, used for analytics, or used for advertising.

Use the Privacy tab to declare the website-content/browsing information required for this user-facing feature, state that it is used only for local hotel-list controls, and provide the privacy-policy URL above.

## Reviewer test instructions

1. Visit a public Booking.com or Agoda hotel search-results page; no account is required.
2. On a hotel result card, select the small blue Hotel View Control button over the hotel image.
3. Choose **Hide** or **Dim**. The result is hidden or visually de-emphasised only in the local browser.
4. Click the extension icon in Chrome’s toolbar to open the saved-preferences panel and choose **Show** to restore a hotel.
5. Use **Clear this site** or **Clear all** to remove stored preferences.
